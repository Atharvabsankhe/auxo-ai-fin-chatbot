# Sample Conversations: AuxoAI Financial Analyst Chatbot

This document showcases 6 example interactions with the chatbot, demonstrating its ability to extract complex financial data and generate professional reports in multiple formats.

---

## 📄 PDF Output Samples

### 1. Risk Factors Summary
*   **Prompt**: "Generate a professional PDF report summarizing the risk factors mentioned in the annual report."
*   **Analyst Action**: Scanned `infosys-ar-25.pdf`, extracted legal, operational, and strategic risks, and formatted them into a multi-page document.
*   **Output**: [Risk_Factors.pdf](file:///Users/atharvasankhe/Desktop/auxo-ai/output/ff8bbd8b_report.pdf)

### 2. Quarterly Performance Executive Summary
*   **Prompt**: "Use the Q1, Q2, Q3, and Q4 press releases to generate a PDF report summarizing the financial performance of each quarter."
*   **Analyst Action**: Aggregated data across 4 different press release PDFs to provide a timeline of fiscal performance.
*   **Output**: [Quarterly_Summary.pdf](file:///Users/atharvasankhe/Desktop/auxo-ai/output/dee98705_report.pdf)

### 3. AI Strategy & Roadmap (Topaz)
*   **Prompt**: "Generate a PDF report on Infosys's AI strategy, specifically focusing on 'Topaz' and their generative AI roadmap as discussed in the annual report."
*   **Analyst Action**: Extracted specific details about Infosys Topaz, generative AI projects (400+ delivered), and their AI-first vision.
*   **Output**: [AI_Roadmap.pdf](file:///Users/atharvasankhe/Desktop/auxo-ai/output/a7990405_report.pdf)

## 📊 Excel Output Samples

### 4. Quarterly Financial Metrics (USD)
*   **Prompt**: "Give me an excel file of quarterly revenue and operating margins for FY25."
*   **Analyst Action**: Extracted tabular data from the internal investor spreadsheet (`investor-sheet.xls`) and converted it into a clean Excel structure.

*   **Prompt**: "Create an Excel file showing the revenue breakdown by business segment (Financial Services, Retail, Communication, etc.) for FY25."
*   **Analyst Action**: Located the segmental reporting tables in the annual report and exported them to Excel.
*   **Output**: [Segment_Breakdown.xlsx](file:///Users/atharvasankhe/Desktop/auxo-ai/output/0a8d0b24_data.xlsx)

### 6. FY24 vs FY25 Comparison
*   **Prompt**: "Generate an Excel comparison of key financial metrics (Revenue, Operating Profit, Net Profit, EPS) between FY24 and FY25."
*   **Analyst Action**: Performed a year-on-year comparison using the consolidated financial statements from the annual report.
*   **Output**: [FY24_vs_FY25.xlsx](file:///Users/atharvasankhe/Desktop/auxo-ai/output/513cfa9c_data.xlsx)
